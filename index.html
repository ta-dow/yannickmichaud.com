About Me

I’m a French Canadian photographer with over 25 years of professional experience. My interest in photography began in a college darkroom, where I met a Danish classmate who would later become my wife. We eventually moved to Copenhagen, where I worked with photography studios and developed a strong foundation in lighting, composition, and image-making. The Scandinavian approach to design continues to influence my work.

My early career focused on beauty and cosmetic photography, including advertising campaigns and editorial work for clients such as Lancôme, L’Oréal, and Givenchy. In 2003, I returned to Canada and took on the technical department of a leading camera company, working across North America. This period also introduced me to wildlife photography and led me to explore photography beyond the studio. Today, my work spans portraiture, flora, landscape, and wildlife photography. I work with both digital and film cameras, including medium-format systems, depending on the subject and approach.

Portrait Photography

Portraiture, my enduring passion, allows me to connect with individuals from all walks of life. Each face tells a unique story, and I relish the challenge of capturing it in black and white. Whether using digital or medium format film cameras, I employ various techniques to create authentic portraits that resonate with viewers.

Flora Photography

This ongoing project explores the details and structures of plants through macro photography. Working at close range reveals forms, textures, and patterns that are easy to overlook in everyday surroundings.

Landscape Photography

Travelling throughout North America has given me the opportunity to photograph a wide range of landscapes, from the mountains of Western Canada to the coast of Newfoundland and the deserts of the American Southwest. Whether capturing a serene scene or crafting a sweeping panorama, I wait patiently for the perfect light to bring my images to life.

Wildlife Photography

Time spent in the Canadian Rockies transformed my career, offering encounters with incredible wildlife such as owls, grizzly bears, and lynx. Patience and a touch of luck reveal nature's wonders in some of the world's most stunning landscapes.
